AI-Driven Job Scheduling in Cloud Computing: A Comprehensive Review
Authors: Sanjalawe et al. (2025), Springer Nature Artificial Intelligence Review.

Cloud computing data centers handle millions of heterogeneous user tasks with variable resource demands and strict quality-of-service constraints. Traditional heuristic scheduling algorithms such as First-Come-First-Served and Round-Robin fail to adapt to bursty workloads and dynamic queueing states.
Machine learning and Reinforcement Learning approaches offer substantial improvements in makespan minimization, energy efficiency, and task deadline compliance.
The authors survey deep Q-networks, policy gradient algorithms, and actor-critic frameworks deployed across enterprise cloud testbeds.
Experimental benchmarks demonstrate that AI-enhanced schedulers reduce task rejection ratios by 28% while improving throughput across virtual machine clusters.
In future iterations, federated scheduling across multi-cloud environments will address strict user data privacy requirements.
